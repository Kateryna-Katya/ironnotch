About the Game

A wooden puzzle designed for thoughtful play.

Nut & Bolt: Screw Woody Puzzle focuses on logic rather than speed. Each level presents a wooden board filled with nuts, bolts, and interlocking pieces that must be removed carefully. The tricky bit is figuring out which bolt to tackle first - mess that up and you're screwed.

As players progress, puzzles gradually introduce more complex arrangements. Some bolts block others, some pieces overlap in unexpected ways, and certain solutions require several steps of planning ahead. Gets tougher as you progress, but it won't make you want to chuck your phone at the wall.

It's pretty relaxing but you still get that 'aha!' moment when you solve it. Instead of rushing through levels, players learn to observe carefully, think ahead, and enjoy the satisfying moment when every piece finally falls into place.

WOODEN MECHANICS

Calm puzzle solving with satisfying mechanics.

The puzzles in Nut & Bolt: Screw Woody Puzzle are designed like miniature mechanical systems. Wooden panels connect through bolts and nuts that hold the entire structure together. Removing a single piece may unlock new paths—or block others if done too soon.

Everything's linked up, so you end up poking around to see how it all fits together. Observing how parts move and interact becomes just as important as choosing which bolt to remove. Each new stage introduces subtle mechanical variations that keep the gameplay fresh.
